## Setup

- Corpus: 204 documents (72 wikipedia, 120 arxiv, 12 mirror) across 6 topics (compilers, computer_vision, cryptography, database_internals, distributed_systems, nlp), indexed as **359 chunks**.
- Embeddings: `voyage-4` (dense) + BM25 with server-side IDF (sparse), in Qdrant.
- Clusters: 8 (spherical k-means, k chosen by cosine silhouette).
- Cluster/topic purity: **81.3%** of chunks fall in a cluster whose majority topic is their own (topic labels are never shown to the clusterer).
- Queries: 290 (140 wikipedia, 120 arxiv, 30 multihop), written by Claude and frozen in `data/eval/queries.jsonl`. Single-hop (`wikipedia`, `arxiv`) queries are written from one sampled passage, whose document is the relevant one. Multi-hop (`multihop`) queries are written from passages of two same-topic articles and need both; both documents are relevant.
- Distractors: the corpus includes near-duplicate `mirror` copies of some articles (lead and headings kept, ~20% of other paragraphs dropped). A mirror is scored as its original, so it earns no extra credit and no extra distinct-document count, but its chunks can crowd other documents out of the top k.
- Metrics use document-level relevance at cutoff k=10: a document counts at the rank of its first chunk. For multi-hop queries, Recall@10 is the fraction of the two relevant documents retrieved.

### Configurations

- **Dense only**: Voyage embeddings, cosine kNN
- **BM25 only**: Sparse BM25, server-side IDF
- **Hybrid (RRF)**: Dense + BM25, reciprocal rank fusion
- **Hybrid + routing (m=1)**: Dense branch restricted to nearest cluster
- **Hybrid + routing (m=3)**: Dense branch restricted to 3 nearest clusters
- **Hybrid + MMR (λ=0.7) (default)**: MMR over the fused candidate pool: the shipped configuration
- **Hybrid + routing + MMR (m=3, λ=0.7)**: Routing and MMR combined

## Caveats

- **Sparse judgments.** Only the source document(s) of each query are judged relevant. Other documents that also answer it count as misses, so absolute scores are a lower bound; comparisons between rows are the meaningful signal.
- **Generated queries.** Each is written from specific passages. Despite the paraphrasing instruction they can share vocabulary with them, which may favor lexical (BM25) matching relative to real user queries.
- **Synthetic distractors.** Mirrors are mechanical copies, more uniform than real-world overlap between documents; they isolate the redundancy effect rather than model it faithfully.
- **MMR is a diversity optimization, not a relevance one.** It can still move nDCG either way: a document counts at the rank of its first chunk, so demoting redundant chunks of other documents can lift the relevant one, while demoting a chunk too similar to one already chosen can push it down. Its intended effect shows in the diversity table; the Δ column shows the net effect on ranking quality.
- Sample size: CIs come from 2,000 bootstrap resamples over queries (seed 0).
